About me

I'm a front-end developer with over 3 years of professional experience, with a focus on building responsive websites and creating interfaces that are both functional and enjoyable to use.

I enjoy solving problems and finding ways to make websites work better, whether that's improving an existing site, fixing a tricky technical issue, or building something from the ground up. My professional experience has given me the opportunity to work across a range of projects and technologies, while my personal projects allow me to explore new tools and continue developing my skills.

I'm always looking to become a stronger all-round front-end developer, expanding my knowledge and taking on new challenges that push me to improve.

The Snap Agency was my first taste of professional working experience as a developer. The Snap agency was a design agency which covered several sectors such as SEO, marketing, design and of course, development. Most of the work I produced was built via page builders such as Salient and Divi with the odd JavaScript solution. I was at The Snap Agency for approximately 1.5 years before moving on. I went in with fundamental knowledge of the 3 pillars of web development (HTML, CSS and JS) and I left with having a working knowledge of how development works in the real world.

After around six months, Think hired a developer with over 15 years of experience who I had the opportunity to work alongside and learn from. They didn't just teach me how to solve individual problems; they taught me how to think as a developer. I learned to look beyond simply making something work and instead consider how it could be made more efficient, intuitive and enjoyable for the end user.

The majority of the websites I have built at Think Design Agency have been developed using WordPress and Advanced Custom Fields (ACF). Using ACF, we create flexible, block-based page structures that allow clients to manage and update their content without having to worry about the underlying code or design. I work on the development of these bespoke themes using HTML, PHP, JavaScript and CSS/SCSS, creating components tailored to each client's requirements.

The projects I have worked on have ranged from simple brochure websites to more complex builds featuring filterable case studies and blog posts, multilingual content and e-commerce functionality using WooCommerce.

My time at Think Design Agency has also given me the opportunity to become much more involved in the wider development lifecycle and communicate directly with clients. As I have become more confident and knowledgeable, I have increasingly been able to answer technical questions independently and contribute to discussions about the technologies and approaches best suited to each project.
